<h2>Ingredients</h2>

- KA bread flour (450g)
- water (320g)
- sea salt (8g)
- sourdough starter (100g)

<h2>Process</h2>

1. Mix water and starter to break apart starter
2. Add flour and salt, mix into shaggy ball
3. Rest for 30 min
4. Stretch and fold 12x, rest for 2 hrs
5. Stretch and fold 6x, rest for 2 hrs
6. Coil folds 4x, rest for 1 hr
7. Preshape, rest for 1hr
8. Shape, overnight cold fermentation
9. Bake:
    - 485F for 20 min
    - 450F for 17 min
